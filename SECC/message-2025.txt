State Employee Charitable Campaign (SECC) is a statewide annual giving campaign that Texas Tech, Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center and the Texas Tech University System participate in each year. This is for TTU employees to make contributions to charity organizations. It is tax deductible.

This year's campaign is from September 3, 2025 to November 26, 2025.
