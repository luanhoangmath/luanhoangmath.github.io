2026 State Employee Charitable Campaign (SECC)

Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Texas Tech University

State Employee Charitable Campaign (SECC) is a statewide annual giving campaign that Texas Tech, Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center and the Texas Tech University System participate in each year. This is for TTU employees to make contributions to charity organizations. It is tax deductible.

The 2026 Campaign: September 2 - November 25, 2026.

Information and instructions.

  1. You can find out more information at TTU SECC page with some Campaign material. The directory of charities is here.
  2. To donate, use TTU account to log in to the TTU's SECC Donation page (or click "Donate Now" on the TTU SECC page)
    Click on "$Make a Pledge" on the top of the right menu to go to the Pledging page.
  3. In the 1st section - Your Information, you can click "Copoy Last Year's Pledge" to reload your charity choices last year. They will appear in "Charities Selection" below.
    Since there are so many organizations, the search function can be very useful.
  4. In the 2nd section - Charities Selection, a list of charity organizations is displayed. You click on the one you want, and it will be added to the list "Charities Selection" below. You can edit the donation amount in your selected list. You can remove, pledge the amount of money for each ...
    *** These charity organizations have to meet strict state criteria to be accepted there. They have websites and information on the list. You can study them more before making the donation. ***
  5. The 3th section - Payment Options. Select your method of payment. Payroll deduction is highly recommended. If so, select how frequently you want to pay from your payroll (12months/9months/...)
    *** It is highly recommended that you use payroll deduction to make contributions through out the year. It will be tax-deductible, and a "small" amount will be deducted from your paycheck. You will not feel any difference!!! ***
  6. The 4th section - Recognition & Acknowledgement. Select whether or how you want their acknowledgement.
  7. Click "Proceed". Check review, summary. Take care of Click "I sign this pledge as a commitment to my selections above." and "Sign".
    Click "Confirm Pledge" to submit, or click "Back" to make changes.
  8. Click "Download Confirmation" or "Return to Home".
I hope that these will help. If you have any questions or need help, please let me know.
